Crispy Springs Community Guide

Welcome to Crispy Springs, a feature-rich community survival server built around exploration, cooperation, meaningful progression and a player-driven economy.

Crispy Springs supports both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition players. You can progress through 550 levels, choose Jobs, build and protect a home, trade with other players, discover hidden treasures, complete achievements, choose whether to participate in optional PvP and create permanent Masterwork equipment through the Community Forge.

Start here

  • Complete the first-join Pathfinder journey.
  • Read and accept the server rules.
  • Collect your starter equipment.
  • Enter Survival and use /rtp to begin exploring.
  • Choose your Jobs and complete daily quests.
  • Protect your first home with a claim.
  • Browse player buy requests with /orders.
  • Apply for the Creator Programme if you make livestreams or gaming content.
  • Use /menu whenever you are unsure what to do next.

Getting Started

Welcome to Crispy Springs! Joining the community is seamless whether you are playing on PC, console, or mobile devices.

Server Connection Details

  • Java Edition IP: 147.135.6.160:25899
  • Bedrock IP: 147.135.6.160 (Port: 25899)

First Steps On Joining

  1. Pathfinder Tutorial: Follow the pathfinder markers around Spawn to learn core mechanics.
  2. Rule Acceptance: Type /rules or click the Lectern at Spawn to review server guidelines.
  3. Starter Kit: Receive your starter tools, food, and initial claim shovel.
  4. Random Teleport: Type /rtp to teleport into a random peaceful area in Survival.

Progression & Leveling

Crispy Springs features a deep 550-level progression ladder that unlocks commands, passive perks, extra homes, vault slots, and exclusive titles.

How Progression Works

  • Type /progress to check your active objectives.
  • Objectives involve leveling Jobs, gaining mcMMO skills, gathering money, and completing daily quests.
  • Upon completing tasks for a level, click Claim Level in /progress to receive your rewards.

Worlds & Navigation

Crispy Springs isolates wilderness harvesting from permanent player settlements to protect the ecosystem.

1. Survival World

The main world for building homes, towns, and shops. Features a massive 1,000,000 × 1,000,000 border where terrain and structures are preserved permanently.

2. Resource World

A dedicated world for gathering raw materials, mining, and deforestation. Access it via /rw tp or through Spawn portals. Note: The Resource World resets periodically!

Economy & Player Market

Our economy is 100% player-driven with zero infinite admin shops inflating the currency.

Earning Currency

  • Jobs: Earn steady cash by mining, farming, fishing, and building.
  • Player Shops: Open chest shops in Survival or set up player warps.
  • Auction House: Post rare items on /ah for global bidding.
  • Marketplace Buy Orders: Sell items directly to players needing supplies via /orders.

Jobs System

Earn income and job experience while playing normally. Join up to 2 jobs initially (unlocked up to 4 via progression).

Available Occupations

  • Miner: Earn money by mining ores and deepslate.
  • Woodcutter: Earn by felling trees and harvesting logs.
  • Farmer: Earn by harvesting and replanting crops.
  • Fisherman: Earn by catching fish and aquatic treasure.
  • Hunter: Earn by defeating hostile mobs and beasts.
  • Builder: Earn by placing decorative blocks and structures.

Use /jobs browse to join an occupation.

mcMMO Skills

Perform natural tasks to level up core RPG skills and unlock powerful active abilities.

Skill Categories

  • Gathering: Mining (Super Breaker), Woodcutting (Tree Feller), Fishing, Herbalism, Excavation.
  • Combat: Swords (Serrated Strikes), Axes (Skull Splitter), Archery, Unarmed, Taming.
  • Agility: Acrobatics (Roll & Graceful Landing).

Type /mcstats to view your skill levels and active perk power level.

Land Claims & Build Protection

Protect your builds, containers, animals, and redstone mechanisms using GriefPrevention claims.

How to Claim Land

  1. Equip a Golden Shovel.
  2. Right-click two opposite corners on the ground to form a diagonal rectangle.
  3. Your claim instantly protects everything from bedrock to build height.

Trust Commands

  • /trust [player] - Gives full permissions to interact and build.
  • /containertrust [player] - Allows opening chests, buttons, and levers.
  • /accesstrust [player] - Allows using doors, buttons, and levers only.
  • /untrust [player] - Revokes permissions.

Homes & Private Vaults

Home Teleports

Save locations to teleport back instantly:

  • /sethome [name] - Saves your location.
  • /home [name] - Teleports to your home after a brief warm-up.
  • /delhome [name] - Deletes a saved home waypoint.

Player Vaults

Access safe virtual chest space anywhere in the server via /pv [number]. Unlock additional vault pages as your progression rank increases.

Player Warps

Create public warps to invite visitors, buyers, or neighbors to your builds, shop centers, or grinders.

Warp Commands

  • /pw - Opens the public warp browser GUI.
  • /pw set [name] - Sets a public warp at your current location.
  • /pw resetlocation [name] - Updates an existing warp to where you stand.
  • /pw remove [name] - Deletes your warp.

Daily Quests & Achievements

Earn daily bonus income, crate keys, and cosmetics by completing rotating daily quests.

  • Type /quests or /odailyquests to view today's active tasks.
  • Quests reset every 24 hours at midnight UTC.
  • Type /achievements to view lifetime achievement milestones.

Community Treasures

Explore Spawn and key public areas to discover over 100 hidden treasure heads!

  • Right-click hidden heads to collect monetary rewards, key fragments, and achievement progress.
  • Track your collected total using the in-game HeadBlocks log.

Community Forge

Craft custom Masterwork equipment with stats beyond standard enchantment limits.

Forge Mechanics

  • Access physical Forge Stations at Spawn or type /forge.
  • Combine rare materials gathered from daily quests and boss mobs to forge Masterwork gear.
  • Masterwork tools feature increased durability, base damage, or unique mining speeds.

Equipment Gems

Enhance your armor and weapons by slotting powerful Equipment Gems.

Gem Types & Slotting

  • Combat Gems: Add lifesteal, critical strike chance, or damage reflection.
  • Utility Gems: Add speed boosts, night vision, or lower hunger depletion.
  • Attach gems to sockets at the Forge Station using /forge.

Graves & Death Recovery

Never worry about losing inventory items to despawning or lava again!

  • Upon death, an **AxGrave** lockbox spawns at your death location containing your items and experience.
  • Graves are protected so only you can unlock your items for 30 minutes.
  • Type /graves to view your death coordinates or navigate back.

Proximity Voice Chat

Interact with nearby players in real-time using position-based audio through **Simple Voice Chat**.

Setup Guide

  1. Install the Simple Voice Chat client mod for Fabric or Forge matching your client version.
  2. Join 147.135.6.160:25899.
  3. Press V in-game to open settings, configure your microphone, and set press-to-talk keybinds.

Chest Shops & Auction House

Setting Up QuickShop Chests

  1. Place a chest down in your land claim.
  2. Hit the chest while holding the item you want to sell.
  3. Type the unit price per item in chat when prompted.

Global Auction House

  • Type /ah to browse active global listings.
  • Sell the item currently in your main hand: /ah sell <price>.

Leaderboards & Rankings

Compete with players across multiple gameplay categories powered by ajLeaderboards.

  • Check physical leaderboard holograms located around Spawn.
  • View stats in-game using /ajlb.
  • Track categories such as **Progression Level**, **Jobs Revenue**, **mcMMO Power Level**, **Economy Balance**, and **Playtime**.

Rules & Community Support

Server Guidelines

  • No Griefing or Stealing: Unclaimed builds are still protected under community rules.
  • Fair Play: X-Ray, fly hacks, auto-clickers, or hacked clients are strictly forbidden and detected automatically by GrimAC.
  • Be Respectful: Keep chat civil and free of hate speech, toxic behavior, or spam.

Getting Support

If you encounter an issue, open a support ticket on our Discord Server.

Marketplace Buy Orders

Need bulk materials for a build? Set up automated buy requests so other players can fill them!

  • Type /orders to open the marketplace interface.
  • Create a buy request specifying the required item, quantity, and payment per item.
  • Sellers can fulfill your order directly through the GUI to receive immediate payment.

Player vs Player (PvP)

PvP is strictly **opt-in** on Crispy Springs so players can build peacefully without fear of surprise attacks.

  • Toggle your PvP status using /pvptoggle.
  • Both combatants must have PvP enabled to deal damage to each other in Survival.

Creator Programme

Do you stream or create YouTube videos, TikToks, or Shorts on Crispy Springs? Apply for our Creator Programme!

  • Receive an in-game LIVE or CREATOR badge.
  • Get stream announcements broadcast to the server chat and Discord automatically.
  • Apply by opening a ticket on our Discord server.

Discord Chat & Account Linking

Link your Minecraft account with our Discord server powered by **DiscordSRV**.

Linking Steps

  1. Type /discord link in-game to receive a unique 4-digit code.
  2. Send a direct message containing the code to the Crispy Springs Discord Bot on Discord.
  3. Your accounts are now linked! You will receive synced roles and secure account recovery support.
